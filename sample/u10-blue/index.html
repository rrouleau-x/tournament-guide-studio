4. Tournament Overview

The Carolina Cup is run by South Carolina United FC and is now in its 26th year. It draws teams from across the Southeast to five complexes across the Columbia and Irmo area, and it has been one of the region's larger youth soccer weekends for a long time. Our U10 team will play 7v7.

What this means for packing

This is a two-wardrobe weekend. Bring the heat gear — water, electrolytes, sunscreen, cooling towels — but also bring a jacket or warm layer for early mornings and a rain shell, because October in the Midlands is not August. Start hydrating the day before travel.

11. Player Guidance

Tournament weekends are exciting and demanding for young players. A few practical reminders.

• Prioritize sleep — early bedtimes Friday and Saturday

• Keep meals familiar — not the weekend for new foods

• Bring simple, preferred snacks

• Encourage steady hydration through the day

• Limit exhausting hotel-pool activity before games

• Bring quiet activities for downtime between games

• Expect physical and emotional fatigue

• Keep postgame conversations positive and brief

• Let coaches handle the soccer instruction

• Emphasize effort, teamwork, learning, and fun

How group ties are decided

Group play is decided on points. The tournament's own results page states that where teams finish level on points it applies its tiebreaker rules in order until the tie is broken, and that anything still level is broken manually by the tournament director. The tiebreaker order itself is only published on that page once games have been played, and the event's rules document sits behind the team login in SincSports Team Documents — so this guide does not print an order borrowed from other events.
